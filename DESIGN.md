# Retro Wedding Invitation

## Overview
A nostalgic, elegant retro wedding invitation interface inspired by vintage Saigon aesthetics, warm color palettes, and delicate decorative elements.

## Colors
- **Primary (#b82323):** Deep Red for key emphasis and titles ("LỄ THÀNH HÔN", "Vũ & Nhím").
- **Background (#fdfbf7):** Vintage Cream for a warm paper feel.
- **Text Dark (#28201c):** Dark Brown for subtle readable sub-text.
- **Accent Gold (#d4af37):** Retro metallic accents and seals.
- **Pastel Purple (#d8c4e8):** Delicate soft purple accents for vintage corner ornaments and borders.

## Components
- **InnerCard:** Rectangular vintage card sliding upward from inside the envelope.
- **WeddingEnvelope:** Outer envelope structure with perspective and flip animations.
